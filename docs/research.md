## Confirmed Event Facts

- Event: Thames Path Ultra Challenge 2026.
- Dates: Saturday 12 September to Sunday 13 September 2026.
- Full route: Putney / Bishops Park to Henley-on-Thames.
- Full distance and elevation: 100 km and about 350 m climb.
- The full route goes upstream from Putney Bridge, past Hampton Court and Runnymede, then on toward Henley-on-Thames.
- The official route is described as the flattest in the Ultra Challenge Series, but still a hard 100 km because of duration, repetitive terrain, and foot stress.
- The confirmation email instructs participants to download the Ultra Challenge app and enter event code `TPC26`.

## Full Continuous Start and Finish

- Start: Bishops Park, Fulham / Putney.
- Finish: Henley-on-Thames.
- Trailblazerz runners: 06:45.
- Runners: from about 07:00.
- Joggers and walkers: about 07:00 to 09:30.
- Ultra March walkers: about 08:30.
- Final start times are provisional and should be confirmed when the organiser publishes event-specific instructions.

## Support and Rest Stops

- Official support stops are every 10-15 km.
- Services include toilets, medics, changing rooms, massage, rest areas, hot drinks, water, snacks, and marquees.
- Key food points for the full challenge:
  - 28 km: picnic lunch and fizzy drinks.
  - 50 km: hot meal.
  - 78 km: brunch or lunch.
  - 100 km: cooked breakfast or hot meal.
  - Finish: glass of fizz, medal, and t-shirt.

## Special Categories

- Ultra March: walking category targeting sub-24 hours for 100 km or sub-12 hours for 50 km.
- Ultra Trailblazerz: competitive runner category. For Thames Path, the stated 100 km target is sub-12 hours for men and sub-13 hours for women; 50 km target is sub-5 hours for men and sub-5.5 hours for women.
- Ultra Challenge says 50 km and 100 km events are ITRA-certified and recognised as UTMB Index races.

## Costs and Funding

The official sign-up page lists these 2026 standard figures, before any platform booking fee or charity-partner discount:

| Funding Option | Full 100 km |
| --- | ---: |
| Self funded | GBP 199 |
| Charity sponsorship | GBP 50 registration fee + GBP 575 minimum fundraising |

| Own-place charity fundraising | GBP 199 + optional fundraising |

The registration system applies a 4% platform booking fee.

## Registration Management

- Registrations are managed through Active Network / My Events.
- The participant area says registered users can view registration details, create or join teams, request funding-type changes, request distance changes, and request place transfers.
- Start-time selection is provisional; the organiser says actual start times are confirmed closer to the event.
