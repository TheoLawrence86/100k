- Event weekend: Saturday 12 September to Sunday 13 September 2026.
- Target format: Full Continuous 100 km.
- Approximate 4-weeks-before point for final start-time confirmation: Saturday 15 August 2026.
- Deadline implied by organiser for booking optional extras, where available: Saturday 22 August 2026.
- Charity 50% fundraising deadline, if using sponsorship or mixed funding: Saturday 22 August 2026.
- Charity remaining fundraising deadline, if applicable: Saturday 10 October 2026.

- [ ] Confirm team name/password if joining or creating a team.

- [ ] Recheck actual start time once released by the organiser.

## Travel

- [ ] Plan travel to Bishops Park / Putney.
- [ ] Note that Putney Bridge tube station is the nearest obvious public-transport option for the start.
- [ ] Do not plan on parking at Bishops Park; the official factsheet says there is no parking at the start.
- [ ] Plan route home from Henley-on-Thames.
- [ ] Decide whether to book organiser shuttle transfers.
- [ ] Build a fallback plan for finishing earlier or later than expected, including a Sunday morning finish.
- [ ] Decide whether someone is meeting at Henley or whether the plan is fully self-supported after the finish.

## Bookable Extras

- [ ] Decide whether any parking, transfer, camping, or meal extras are needed.
- [ ] Ignore 2-day camping unless plans change; full continuous does not use the planned overnight split.
- [ ] Check 100 km baggage transfer options: start to halfway to finish, or start to finish.
- [ ] Prepare cash/card for any baggage services that are paid on the day.
- [ ] Reconfirm all extras by Saturday 22 August 2026.

## Kit and Nutrition

- [ ] Build a shoe and sock plan.
- [ ] Decide whether to send spare shoes and socks to Runnymede / halfway.
- [ ] Test waterproofs, head torch, backup light, charging, layers, and blister kit.
- [ ] Test all race-day nutrition during long sessions.
- [ ] Check organiser food stops against personal needs; plan backup calories.
- [ ] Prepare a night section plan for the second half.
- [ ] Test the exact pack/vest weight expected on event day.
- [ ] Confirm whether poles are useful, trained, and allowed by final event instructions.
